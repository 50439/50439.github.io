Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Channel Uploader ("the tool") is used only by the owner of the YouTube channels Chronicle Lens and Nature Unfolds to manage those channels.

What data the tool accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the tool uses Google OAuth to access the owner's YouTube channels: uploading and scheduling videos, editing video titles, descriptions, tags and playlists, and reading channel and video statistics.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish and manage the owner's own videos. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored privately by the owner and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The tool does not collect data about viewers or other YouTube users.

Google API Services

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tool uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact
